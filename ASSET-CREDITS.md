# Neon City

A mobile-first, original 3D city exploration prototype.

## External 3D assets and licenses

- **Kenney City Kit (Commercial) and City Kit (Roads)** — CC0 1.0. Models are served from the public `Hidencod/tge-assets` mirror of Kenney assets.
  - https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial
  - https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads
  - https://github.com/Hidencod/tge-assets
- **Soldier.glb** — animated humanoid model from the official Three.js examples repository. Three.js repository is MIT-licensed.
  - https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js
  - https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/LICENSE

## Controls

- Left joystick: move
- Drag the 3D view: rotate camera
- Run: hold the run button
- Jump: tap jump
- Map: open the city map

The game loads the GLB models from public CDNs at runtime, so an internet connection is required for the external assets.
